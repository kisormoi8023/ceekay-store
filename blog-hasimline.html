Vendor Spotlight

Hasimline: Tailored for the Modern African Man

Handcrafted menswear, now featured on Ceekay

Back to Blog Vendor Spotlight · Menswear

At Ceekay we love finding makers who bring African heritage into everyday modern style. This month we are shining the spotlight on Hasimline, a menswear label whose motto says it all: tailored for the modern African man.

Who is Hasimline?

Hasimline creates native and contemporary outfits for men who want to look sharp for every occasion, from Friday prayers and weddings to the office and weekend outings. Every piece is cut with attention to fit, clean finishing and fabrics that feel as good as they look.

Why we love them

  • Tailored fit: silhouettes cut to flatter, not swamp.
  • Heritage meets modern: classic senator, kaftan and agbada styles with a fresh edge.
  • Easy ordering: shop online and check out straight through WhatsApp.
