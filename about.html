Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ceekay Store collects, why, and how we protect it. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect +

Account information: name, email address, password (stored as a one-way hash — we never see or store your actual password), and shipping address.

Order information: the products, quantities, and totals of orders you place.

Payment information: card details are entered directly into Stripe's secure checkout page and never touch our servers — we only store the order total and Stripe's payment reference.

Automatically collected information: standard web log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) and cookies, described below.

2. How we use your information +
  • Process and deliver your orders, and contact you about them.
  • Create and manage your account, and let you log back in securely.
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, password resets).
  • Send marketing/newsletter emails, only if you opted in at sign-up.
  • Improve the site and troubleshoot problems.
3. Cookies & advertising +

Essential cookies: we use a login session cookie so you can stay signed in. This is required for the site to function and can't be turned off without logging you out.

Advertising (Meta Pixel): we use the Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) to measure the performance of our ads and show you relevant ads on those platforms. It records events like viewing a product, adding to cart, or completing a purchase. You can control how Meta uses this data in your Ad Preferences, or block it site-wide with a browser tracker-blocking extension.

4. Sharing your information +

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what's needed to run the store, with:

  • Stripe — to process payments securely.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — pixel event data, for ad measurement as described above.
  • Our email provider — to send order and account emails.
5. Data security & retention +

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and can't be reversed, even by us. We keep account and order records for as long as your account is active, or as needed to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

6. Your rights & choices +

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, by emailing ceekay.customersupport@gmail.com.

7. Children's privacy +

Ceekay Store is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy +

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact us +

Questions about this policy? Email ceekay.customersupport@gmail.com.

Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Thank you for shopping with Ceekay. If you are not entirely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help.

1. Returns +

You have 30 calendar days to return an item from the date you received it.

To be eligible for a return, your item must:

  • Be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in the same condition that you received it.
  • Be in its original packaging with all tags still attached.
  • Include the receipt, order confirmation, or other proof of purchase.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Gift cards
  • Final-sale or clearance items
  • Intimate apparel, swimwear, or hygiene-sensitive products where the seal has been broken
2. Refunds +

Once we receive your returned item, our team will inspect it and notify you that we have received your package. We will then notify you of the status of your refund.

Approved returns: If your return is approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.

Processing time: Your credit should appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer’s policies.

3. Exchanges +

We replace items only when they are defective, damaged, or when you need a different size. To request an exchange for the same product, contact us at ceekay.customersupport@gmail.com and follow the return instructions we provide.

4. Shipping Costs +

You are responsible for return shipping costs. Shipping costs are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, the cost of the initial shipping may be deducted from your refund.

For items valued at more than $50, we recommend using a trackable shipping service or purchasing shipping insurance.

5. Damaged or Defective Items +

If you receive a damaged or defective product, contact us immediately at ceekay.customersupport@gmail.com with your order number and clear photos of the damage. We will process a replacement or full refund at no extra cost to you.

6. Contact Us +

If you have questions about returning an item, contact us at ceekay.customersupport@gmail.com.
